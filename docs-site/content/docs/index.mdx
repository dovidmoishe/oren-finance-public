Oren connects your AI tools to your portfolio and tokenized stocks on Solana. Use the MCP server to look up stocks, read your holdings and prepare trades with your permission.

## Get started

Connect Oren to your client:

- [Claude](/docs/mcp/claude)
- [Cursor](/docs/mcp/cursor)
- [Codex](/docs/mcp/codex)

New to MCP? Follow the [quickstart](/docs/mcp/quickstart).

## Explore

Browse the [public tools](/docs/tools), read the [MCP tool reference](/docs/mcp/tools), and learn how [permissions](/docs/authentication) work.

The [developer SDK](/docs/sdk) is coming later. For now, start with MCP.
